A LETTER FROM MY HEART

Happy Birthday, Akka! ❤️

Sometimes, the most beautiful relationships begin in the most unexpected ways. And ours is one of those beautiful stories.

I still remember how everything started. We both wanted to talk to each other, but we didn't really know each other. Then, on my birthday, I asked Nani to introduce us. I never imagined that one small introduction by Nani would become the beginning of such a beautiful bond between us.

From that day, slowly, we started getting closer. Those endless conversations, talking until midnight, sharing little things about our lives, laughing together and discussing almost everything... those moments became some of my favourite memories. Without even realizing it, you became someone very special to me.

Akka, I want you to know something. You are not just someone I got introduced to through a relationship; you have become someone I genuinely love, respect and feel connected to. I'm so grateful that life gave me a sister who feels so special to my heart.

I know that life isn't always easy. You have responsibilities, dreams, emotions and so many things that you carry in your heart. But I want you to remember one thing: You are stronger than you sometimes think, and you are capable of achieving so many beautiful things.

You are already a wonderful mother to your little one, and I hope you always remember how precious and important you are. As you dream of welcoming another little blessing, especially the baby girl you wish for, I pray that your journey is filled with happiness, good health, peace and countless beautiful moments. May your family be blessed with all the love it deserves.

And Akka, please never forget yourself while taking care of everyone else. Your dreams matter too. Your happiness matters too. You deserve to smile freely, enjoy life, follow your dreams and experience all the beautiful things you wish for.

Whenever life feels difficult, don't lose hope. Take one step at a time. Believe in yourself. You don't have to be strong every single moment. It's okay to feel tired, it's okay to have difficult days, but never think that those days define your entire life. Better and brighter days are always worth hoping for.

I may not be able to solve every problem in your life, but I promise that I'll always be someone who listens to you, supports you, motivates you and celebrates your happiness.

No matter how our journey started, I'm so happy that Nani introduced us and that it brought us together. I don't know what beautiful memories are waiting for us in the future, but I hope we continue our midnight conversations, silly laughs, little secrets and endless sisterly love for many more years.

On your special day, I wish you a lifetime of happiness, good health, strength, success and all the blessings your heart is waiting for.

Keep smiling, keep dreaming and keep believing in yourself, Akka. You deserve a beautiful life.

And always remember, you have a little sister who is genuinely happy to have you in her life and who wishes to share many more beautiful sister moments with you.

With endless love,

Your Little Sister ♡

Not every sisterhood begins with childhood memories. Some begin with a simple introduction and grow into a forever bond. Ours is one of them. 🥹❤️
